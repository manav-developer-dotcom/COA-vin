01 / CONCEPT

Algorithm Overview

What is Booth's Algorithm?

Booth's algorithm is a signed binary multiplication technique used in digital computers. It uses 2's complement arithmetic and checks two adjacent bits of the multiplier to decide whether addition, subtraction or no operation is required.


Decision Table

00

No operation

01

Add M to A

10

Subtract M from A

11

No operation


Following the selected operation, an arithmetic right shift is applied to the combined A, Q and Q-1 registers. The process continues for n iterations.
